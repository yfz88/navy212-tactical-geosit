I. Data & Model Preparation (Back-End)

1. Collect Observations

Satellites, floats, gliders, ships, UUVs (e.g., NOAA, NASA)

PAIN: Sparse deep-ocean data.
2. QA/QC & Processing

Clean, standardize, and format data (NAVO, NRL, NUWC)

3. Modeling & Data Assimilation

Build ocean forecasts, climatologies, and 2D environmental models from assimilated data.

PAIN: Uncertainty in inputs.
4. Validation & Uncertainty Estimation

Quantify environmental variance and generate distinct uncertainty layers (e.g., ensemble spreads, conformal predictions)

GAIN: Quantify sonar-specific uncertainty.

Sonar Operator

USE CASE A:
MISSION PLANNING
A1. Analyze Historical Data & Preloaded Maps
  • Identify search areas and inspect pre-mission environmental climatology.
A2. Simulate Hypothetical Scenarios & Run TDAs
  • Test hypotheticals (speed, depth, transmission loss) to predict sonar performance ranges.
PAIN: TDA trust is low; models are static and cannot recompute on the fly.
A3. Develop Briefs
  • Draft mission briefs and schedule environmental sensor deployments for Command.
GAIN: Clearer pre-mission awareness of where environmental uncertainty will impact sensor performance.
USE CASE B:
MISSION EXECUTION
B1. Conduct Real-Time Environmental In-Situ Sampling
  • Deploy local probes and conduct sound speed casts to gather real-time temperature and salinity data.
B2. Target "Best Beams" Toward Areas of Greatest Uncertainty
  • Analyze sound speed and bottom loss, and steer high-gain arrays toward degraded or uncertain bearings to reject background noise.
GAIN: Maximizes array gain and signal-to-noise ratio where environmental data is least reliable.
B3. Detect, Classify, Localize, & Track (DCLT)
  • Evaluate live sonar returns against environmental models to distinguish actual targets from biological noise and ocean clutter.
PAIN: Cognitive overload; no system to log false positives.
B4. Adapt Asset Placement On-the-Fly
  • Fine-tune sensors, adjust tactical parameters, and update local positioning as environmental conditions shift under limited comms.
GAIN: Confidence overlay directs operators' limited attention.

Actionable Command Decisions
